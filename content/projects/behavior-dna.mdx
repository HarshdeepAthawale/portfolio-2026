Built for the CBI Hackathon. Passwords prove what you know and OTPs prove what you have — Behavior DNA proves **who you are**.

## The idea

Stolen credentials and SIM-swaps pass every traditional check. Behavior DNA watches *how* a user types and touches from the first keypress, so an attacker holding the right password still looks wrong.

## How it works

- An **on-device SDK** captures 40+ keystroke, touch and session signals and flushes them every 500 ms. Device identifiers are HMAC-hashed on the device and never leave in the clear.
- A **FastAPI decision engine** evaluates every login through three parallel pathways: behavioral machine learning, blockchain-anchored device trust, and coercion context.
- Each login returns **APPROVE / STEP_UP / HARD_BLOCK** in under 80 ms, with the top **SHAP** attributions behind every decision.

## Security features

- **SIM-swap defense** — a device-hash baseline on Hyperledger Fabric; a mismatch is blocked outright.
- **Duress protection** — a silent duress PIN opens a decoy session and alerts trusted contacts.
- **Coercion detection** — screen-sharing, hesitation and paste patterns raise the risk score.
- **Post-quantum crypto** — ML-KEM-768 key encapsulation and ML-DSA-65 signatures (FIPS 203/204).
- **Active deception** — honeypot fields and decoy endpoints that fingerprint intruders.
